# Health & Wellness OS

> An evidence-sovereign operating system for improving human health through root-cause analysis, biological congruence, low-toxicity intervention, structured experimentation, and honest uncertainty.

## Mission

Build a living health intelligence system that can discover the best available path without outsourcing truth to any single institution, industry, medical tribe, country, or charismatic practitioner.

The OS is designed to resist two symmetrical failure modes:

1. **Institutional capture** — confusing regulatory approval, consensus, prestige, or funding volume with truth.
2. **Alternative-health capture** — confusing natural origin, testimonials, suppressed-cure narratives, or mechanistic plausibility with demonstrated human benefit.

The objective is not conventional medicine, alternative medicine, or naturopathy as an identity. The objective is **evidence-sovereign ecological medicine**: restore the conditions under which the body can regulate and repair itself, while using targeted interventions when their expected benefit justifies their total risk.

## Core doctrine

- Root cause before chronic compensation.
- Remove insults before adding interventions.
- Restore missing physiological inputs before overriding pathways.
- Prefer the lowest-disruption intervention capable of solving the problem.
- Treat lived experience and traditional use as discovery evidence, not automatic proof.
- Audit funding, incentives, denominators, adverse-event capture, and publication bias in every direction.
- Separate mechanistic, cell, animal, observational, controlled-human, and long-term evidence.
- Evaluate complete strategies, not isolated ingredients.
- Make risk proportional to disease severity and opportunity cost.
- Preserve uncertainty instead of collapsing everything into “proven” or “debunked.”

## Health systems map

| System | Primary objective | First-line levers |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence & measurement | Know what is actually happening | Baselines, labs when useful, exposure history, symptom tracking, standardized photos, N-of-1 experiments |
| Sleep & circadian rhythm | Restore recovery and hormonal timing | Consistent sleep/wake time, morning light, dark/cool nights, sleep-disorder screening |
| Nutrition | Supply building blocks without chronic metabolic overload | Whole foods, adequate protein and micronutrients, individualized trigger testing |
| Metabolic health | Maintain insulin sensitivity and fuel flexibility | Resistance training, walking, meal timing, body-composition stability, context-dependent fasting |
| Movement | Preserve strength, aerobic capacity, mobility, and insulin sensitivity | Strength, zone 2, vigorous work, mobility, daily walking |
| Nervous system & stress | Reduce chronic allostatic load | Breathwork, prayer/meditation, nature, relationships, workload redesign, trauma-informed support |
| Environmental load | Reduce unnecessary biological insults | Air/water quality, occupational exposures, personal-care and household-product audit |
| Gut, oral health & microbiome | Preserve digestion, absorption, barrier integrity, and ecological balance | Oral hygiene, fiber/food diversity as tolerated, infection and malabsorption assessment |
| Infection & parasites | Detect and remove persistent biological burdens | Exposure-based screening, organism-specific testing, targeted treatment, reinfection prevention |
| Skin & hair | Improve barrier, inflammation, collagen resilience, and appearance | Gentle cleansing, nutrition/sleep, exposure control, minimal targeted actives |
| Immune regulation | Improve defense without chronic overactivation | Sleep, nutrition, exposure reduction, infection control, condition-specific evaluation |
| Social connection & meaning | Support longevity, regulation, adherence, and purpose | Family, friendship, service, faith, community, meaningful work |
| Clinical escalation | Prevent avoidable harm in severe or time-sensitive disease | Diagnosis, second opinions, risk-benefit comparison, integrative support, emergency care when necessary |

See [`docs/02-health-system-map.md`](docs/02-health-system-map.md) for the expanded architecture.

## Decision sequence

1. Define the outcome precisely.
2. Establish severity and time sensitivity.
3. Map likely upstream causes and exposures.
4. Remove obvious insults.
5. Restore foundational inputs.
6. Measure response.
7. Add one low-risk targeted intervention at a time where possible.
8. Escalate only when expected benefit exceeds total harm and delay risk.
9. Record the result, including failures and adverse effects.
10. Update the OS.

## Evidence states

Every claim should be labeled as one of:

- **Established**
- **Strongly supported**
- **Promising**
- **Mechanistically plausible**
- **Traditional / experiential signal**
- **Anecdotally interesting**
- **Contradicted**
- **Unknown / under-researched**

## Safety boundary

This repository is a research and decision-support system, not a substitute for individualized diagnosis or urgent care. The OS explicitly distinguishes low-risk self-experiments from interventions where the wrong choice, dose, interaction, or delay could cause serious harm.
