# Health & Wellness OS Research Backlog

This backlog converts broad health intuitions into falsifiable, decision-relevant research programs.

## Priority model

Score each topic on:

- **Expected health impact**
- **Probability current conventional practice is suboptimal**
- **Probability alternative claims are inflated**
- **Personal relevance**
- **Time sensitivity**
- **Research tractability**
- **Risk if wrong**

## P0 — build the operating infrastructure

### 1. Personal health baseline and measurement OS

Deliverables:

- medical and exposure history template;
- current symptoms and goals;
- sleep, nutrition, movement, stress, and environment baseline;
- medication/supplement/product inventory;
- laboratory decision map rather than indiscriminate testing;
- weekly and monthly review templates;
- intervention log with start/stop rules.

### 2. Intervention registry

Each intervention record should contain:

- purpose;
- evidence state;
- dose/preparation;
- expected mechanism;
- benefits and harms;
- interactions;
- personal response;
- stop rule;
- source and incentive audit.

### 3. Source and claim database

Track:

- exact claim;
- evidence layer;
- country/institution;
- funding;
- conflicts;
- replication;
- outcome quality;
- confidence changes over time.

## P1 — immediate personal leverage

### Skincare and post-weight-loss facial appearance

Questions:

- How much of the concern is surface quality versus facial-volume redistribution?
- What changes after several months of stable weight?
- Which routine maximizes barrier quality, collagen resilience, and appearance with minimal exposure?
- What is the best internationally sourced vitamin C formulation?
- How do Korean practices separate real leverage from marketing and multi-step consumption?
- How do bakuchiol, retinal/retinoids, azelaic acid, green tea, niacinamide, and mineral protection compare on impact, irritation, systemic exposure, and evidence?
- Which products are fragrance-free, transparent, and independently tested?

Deliverable: `protocols/skincare-os.md`

### Hair and scalp health

Questions:

- What is the likely phenotype: androgenetic loss, inflammatory scalp disease, traction, nutritional issue, stress shedding, or mixed?
- Which non-minoxidil approaches have meaningful human evidence?
- How should microneedling, PRP, light therapy, caffeine, rosemary, peptides, and nutritional correction be ranked?
- Which topical ingredients create pet toxicity risk?

### Sustainable body composition after major weight loss

Questions:

- What weight/body-fat range optimizes face, energy, strength, and metabolic markers?
- How quickly does facial appearance stabilize after a large weight change?
- How can lean mass be increased without repeating an aggressive bulk-cut cycle?
- What protein, calorie, and resistance-training system is sustainable?

### Sleep and breathing

Questions:

- Is sleep quality limited by schedule, nasal breathing, apnea risk, stress, caffeine, or environment?
- What is the minimum diagnostic path for sleep-disordered breathing?
- Which non-pharmaceutical interventions have the highest expected value?

### Oral and periodontal health

Questions:

- How strongly do periodontal disease, mouth breathing, and untreated dental infection affect systemic inflammation?
- Which oral-care practices preserve the microbiome while controlling disease?
- What are the tradeoffs of antiseptic mouthwash, hydroxyapatite, fluoride, xylitol, oil pulling, and oral probiotics?

### Mold, air, water, and household exposures

Questions:

- Which exposures have high-certainty harm versus speculative trace-level concern?
- What testing is reliable for water, mold, particulate matter, radon, metals, and occupational chemicals?
- What is the 80/20 non-toxic household conversion sequence?

## P2 — metabolic and longevity research

### Fasting and autophagy

Questions:

- Which human tissues show measurable autophagy changes at which fasting durations?
- What benefits are attributable to fasting itself versus calorie reduction or weight loss?
- How do 16-hour, 24-hour, 36-hour, 48-hour, and longer fasts compare?
- How do resistance training, protein intake, electrolytes, sleep, sex, and body composition modify effects?
- What are the lean-mass, gallstone, arrhythmia, refeeding, and hormonal risks?
- Who should not fast?

Deliverable: a phenotype-specific fasting decision tree, not a universal schedule.

### Metabolic health beyond weight

Questions:

- Which markers best capture insulin resistance and metabolic flexibility?
- What is the impact hierarchy among sleep, walking, strength, whole-food diet, meal timing, and carbohydrate restriction?
- Which people benefit from low-carb or ketogenic diets, and which do not?

### Supplements and nutrient sufficiency

Questions:

- Which deficiencies are common enough to screen or supplement empirically?
- Which supplements have clear outcome evidence rather than biomarker-only effects?
- Which combinations create interaction or contamination risk?
- How should creatine, vitamin D, magnesium, omega-3, collagen, glycine, zinc, copper, and B vitamins be personalized?

## P2 — medicine and incentive architecture

### Pharmaceutical versus root-cause intervention taxonomy

Build categories based on biological action rather than ideology:

- restoration/replacement;
- nature-derived targeted agent;
- physiological modulator;
- broad suppressor;
- cytotoxic intervention;
- antimicrobial/antiparasitic;
- regenerative or root-aligned intervention;
- experimental repurposed agent;
- cosmetic or symptom-compensating intervention.

For each, map expected potency, selectivity, reversibility, monitoring burden, and appropriate disease severity.

### Medical research incentive map

Questions:

- Which intervention types are structurally underfunded?
- How do patents, reimbursement, publication, liability, and regulatory pathways shape standard care?
- How frequently do independent studies reverse sponsor-favorable conclusions?
- How can unpatentable interventions receive rigorous testing?

### Traditional and distributed-experience evidence system

Build a registry that captures:

- culture and lineage;
- indication;
- preparation and dose;
- number treated;
- response and nonresponse;
- adverse events;
- objective measures;
- duration of follow-up;
- commercial conflicts;
- proposed modern test.

## P3 — serious-disease frontier

### Cancer metabolic and ecological medicine

This requires the highest evidence discipline because opportunity cost is extreme.

Research tracks:

- Warburg effect and tumor metabolic heterogeneity;
- ketogenic diets by cancer phenotype and cachexia risk;
- fasting or fasting-mimicking approaches around treatment;
- insulin/IGF signaling;
- exercise and muscle preservation;
- sleep and circadian disruption;
- microbiome and treatment response;
- repurposed drugs including fenbendazole/mebendazole hypotheses;
- hyperthermia and oxygenation;
- integrative-clinic outcome transparency;
- spontaneous regression case reconstruction;
- treatment toxicity minimization without sacrificing cure probability.

Non-negotiable rule: never generalize a cell-culture effect to a human cure claim without dose, tissue exposure, safety, and outcome evidence.

### Autoimmune and chronic inflammatory disease

Questions:

- What proportion is driven or modified by infection, barrier dysfunction, diet, environment, sleep, stress, or deficiency?
- Which removal/restoration interventions alter disease trajectory rather than only symptoms?
- When is immunosuppression necessary to prevent irreversible damage?

### Cardiometabolic disease

Questions:

- Which patients can reverse hypertension or diabetes through lifestyle and weight loss?
- Which phenotypes require medication despite strong lifestyle adherence?
- What are the best withdrawal/deprescribing protocols after measurable improvement?

## Standard for completion

A module is not complete until it contains:

- precise claims;
- international source diversity;
- traditional/lived-experience layer;
- mechanism and dose analysis;
- sponsor and incentives audit;
- benefits, harms, and opportunity cost;
- phenotype-specific decision tree;
- falsification criteria;
- practical next action.
