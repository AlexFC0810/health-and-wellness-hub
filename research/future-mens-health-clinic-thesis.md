# Future venture thesis: evidence-led men's health & longevity clinic

_Status: long-horizon venture option (approximately 2031–2036); exploratory, not an active launch or commitment. Added 2026-10-08._

## Thesis
There may be a durable business in **trusted, measurable, longitudinal men's health care** rather than a conventional "prescribe testosterone to anyone who wants to optimize" clinic. The advantage is not a branded injection. It is better diagnosis, continuity, transparent outcomes and stronger clinical governance, with technology reducing administrative cost.

## Demand hypothesis to validate
Some customers seek better energy, recovery, body composition, sexual health, fertility awareness and preventive care, but face fragmented diagnostics, opaque prices, confusing results, and sales-led optimization claims. Hypothesis only — validate customer outcomes, willingness to pay, and clinical pathways separately.

## Comparators to study — not endorsements
- **Ways2Well**, founded by **Brigham Buhler**: lab-led wellness and subscription care, practitioner visits, and optional clinical treatments. https://ways2well.com/
- **Overtime Men's Health / OTMENS**, co-founded by **Julian Becerra**: men's hormone, peptide, diagnostics and telemedicine positioning. https://www.otmens.com/
- Independent due diligence is essential: Business Insider (2025-08-30, updated 2025-09-19) reported Overtime had offered unapproved retatrutide; Becerra later said it stopped due to legal concerns. Do not presume this is still offered. This historical concern underscores diligence on present-day product sourcing and clinical governance. https://www.businessinsider.com/eli-lilly-retatrutide-gray-market-fitness-circles-before-launch-2025-8

## Differentiation
1. **Evidence-sovereign diagnostics**: accurate repeat measurement, symptoms, follow-up and alternative-cause evaluation before prescribing.
2. **Fertility-aware care**: explicit preservation priorities, appropriate referrals to urology/reproductive endocrinology and informed options.
3. **Clinical governance by licensed professionals**: independently licensed prescribers, proper medical director/ownership and state-by-state compliance. Business founder does not perform diagnosis or prescribe without the legal credentials.
4. **Outcome accountability**: track symptoms, patient-reported outcomes, adverse effects, monitoring adherence and patient retention without artificially pursuing maximal hormone numbers.
5. **Operational compounding**: AI for intake, scheduling, results navigation, reminders, care coordination and privacy-compliant administration, never unsupervised clinical prescribing.

## Regulatory and ethical red lines
- Do not market unsupported testosterone claims (e.g., guaranteed income, cognitive superiority, longevity).
- Do not equate a compounded product with FDA approval or misstate research-only peptide safety.
- Verify medical practice ownership structure, telemedicine licensure, prescribing and pharmacy rules, HIPAA obligations and advertising compliance in chosen states before a launch.
- Clinical quality and patient welfare take precedence over prescribing volume and subscription conversion.

## Low-cost research phase; no immediate clinic build
- Study clinical evidence, care gaps and provider models.
- Shadow/interview qualified clinicians and patients with informed consent; no personal health data in this repository.
- Compare cash-pay diagnostics/visit economics and patient-outcome quality.
- Prototype **nonclinical** workflow infrastructure only if a real provider or buyer requests it.
- Revisit the launch decision when capital, partnerships, licensing, outcome data and timing justify it.

## Go / no-go gate in ~5–10 years
Proceed only if there is qualified clinical leadership, a legally viable model, validated payer willingness, responsible sourcing, clear evidence-based protocols, strong privacy/security, and a differentiated cost/outcome proposition. Otherwise keep this as a research option.
